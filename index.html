A few things you might be wondering

The little details.

Who is Vow & Found designed for?

Engaged couples in Ireland planning their own wedding to a set budget, and independent wedding suppliers who want an easy-to-understand listing. The couple uses the discovery tools for free; the supplier pays the proposed membership.

Do I need to show my prices?

Yes. The proposed service asks each supplier for an indicative starting price and a clear description of what it covers. Couples filter within their chosen category. A starting price is not a fixed quote; travel, guest numbers and upgrades can change the final cost.

What can I actually try in this preview?

Open the couple demo, choose a category, set a budget, view supplier profiles and save a shortlist. You can simulate an enquiry and mark a sample supplier as booked. These changes stay in this browser; no real supplier is contacted.

Can I join and start receiving bookings now?

No. This is a fictitious student project. The registration form demonstrates a supplier's journey only. Real accounts, listing verification, enquiries, subscriptions and confirmed bookings would need a secure production service.

About this student project — problem, requirements, evidence & reflection

Fictitious concept. The project uses invented suppliers, contacts, enquiries and prices. Public stock photography is illustrative only. No real customer, colleague or employer information is used.

Area 1 · Think

The problem and the people.

Specific segment: Engaged couples in Ireland who are planning their own wedding, without a full-service planner, and need to choose multiple suppliers within a fixed budget. Couples decide and use the service day to day. Independent suppliers are the proposed paying customers at €30/month.

“I don't want to contact ten photographers just to find out none of them are in our budget.”Invented illustrative quote — not interview evidence.

Current workaround — an assumption to validate: Couples use directories, social media and individual websites, request prices separately, and keep their own notes or spreadsheet of replies and bookings. The directories below provide real evidence of an existing supplier-discovery market; they do not validate demand for this specific new service.

Six checkable user requirements

  1. Choose a supplier category before seeing budget controls.
  2. See starting prices and filter that category by a maximum price.
  3. Narrow suppliers by an Irish county.
  4. Open package details and sample contact information.
  5. Save suppliers, track enquiries and mark simulated bookings.
  6. Complete these tasks on a phone without horizontal scrolling.

Two existing services — checked at source on 28 October 2026

weddingsonline — supplier directory
Provides category and county browsing, supplier reviews and enquiries. It already solves part of supplier discovery.

Hitched Ireland — wedding suppliers
Provides category browsing, supplier profiles and pricing requests. Some listings show starting prices; the site also offers planning tools.

Proposed distinction, not a proven unique invention: Each service category has its own remembered budget and a consistent sample package summary, paired with a small planning dashboard. Wedding directories, prices and planners already exist; interviews and further competitive testing are needed to establish a meaningful difference.

Area 2 · Build

A category-first wedding marketplace.

A responsive website and browser-based planning tracker. The journey is choose a category → set a budget and compare → save and track. All interactive features are implemented in client-side JavaScript.

Sustainability assumption: Supplier membership is €30/month; couples browse free. At an illustrative 50 paying suppliers, gross monthly revenue would be €1,500 before all costs. Customer acquisition, support, infrastructure and taxes have not been costed, so this is not a profitability forecast.

Area 3 · Working demo

Try the couple experience in one click. Select Photographers, set a €1,500 maximum, open a profile, save it, simulate an enquiry and visit My planner. No real reservation or message is created.

Area 4 · Presenting the concept

The supplier-facing landing page explains the benefits and proposed membership. The couple-facing demo uses restrained sage, blush, cream and warm neutrals, clear category choices and pricing controls placed only where they are relevant.
